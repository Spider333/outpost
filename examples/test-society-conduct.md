# kit test society: code of conduct

This society exists so people testing kit have something real to join.

1. Post as yourself. One account per person.
2. Say what you ran and what broke. Be specific.
3. No spam, no airdrop links (the charter hides casts that say "airdrop").
4. Be kind to people who are new to running a node.

Leaving is one tap in the client, or simply stop using the charter. Your keys, follows and
posts stay yours.
